This notice tells you exactly what data the pilot collects, where it goes, how long we

## What we collect, and where it lives

| Data | Where it is processed / stored | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Spender's messages with steward | Apple (iMessage), Linq (message delivery), our steward database, and OpenAI (the AI provider whose model reads and replies to messages) | Running the assistant; studying how conversations go |
| Steward's decisions and system events | Our steward database (event log) | The core research record |
| Spending rules the sponsor sets | Our steward database | Running the policy engine |
| Product/price data steward looks up | Our steward database / logs | Reconstructing decisions |
| Names and phone numbers | A separate contact store, not in the research data | Contacting you; connecting the phone |
| Interview and check-in notes; recordings only if you agree | Operator's private storage | The research findings |

**No real payment details exist anywhere in this pilot.** The money is simulated; we
never ask for or store card numbers, bank details, or payment credentials.

## How the research data is kept

Research records (the event log) use a code number for your pair, not your name. The
list connecting code numbers to names and phone numbers is stored separately and is
deleted first.

## Third parties, honestly stated

Your messages necessarily pass through Apple (iMessage), Linq (the messaging service),
and OpenAI (the AI provider). Each holds data under its own published retention
policies, which we do not control. We will not send your data to anyone else, and we
will not sell it or use it for advertising or AI training of our own.

## How long we keep it

- Raw message content: deleted **[30] days after the pilot ends**.
- Interview recordings: deleted once transcribed.
- Pseudonymised event log and analysis: kept for the research write-up.
- Contact details: deleted at the end of the retention period, or sooner on request.

## Your choices

- **Withdraw from the pilot**: any time, no reason needed — text STOP to steward or
  contact us. Effective within 24 hours.
- **Have your data deleted**: separately from withdrawing, you can ask us to delete
  your message content and identifying details. You can withdraw and let the data
  stand, or withdraw and have it deleted — your choice.
- **Honest limits**: we cannot delete the iMessage history on your own phone or the
  other person's, and data already held by Apple, Linq, or OpenAI follows their
  policies. Deletion on our side covers everything we hold.
