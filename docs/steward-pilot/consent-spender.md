## What steward is

Steward is an experimental, AI-powered assistant you reach by iMessage. You text it
about things you need, and it checks each request against spending rules your sponsor
set, then tells you whether it's approved.

**Important: no real money moves in this pilot.** Steward runs on pretend ("sandbox")
money. Nothing is actually bought and nothing will arrive. We are testing whether the
conversations and decisions make sense — not doing real shopping.

## What you will do

- A short call with us to set things up and connect your phone.
- Text steward when you'd like to, plus a couple of specific things we'll ask you to
  try (for example, requesting something you expect it to say no to).
- A short private check-in call with us mid-way, and a 30-minute chat at the end.

You will be paid [AMOUNT] for your time, however much or little you use it.

## Who sees your messages — read this part carefully

- **Your sponsor does NOT see your conversation with steward.** Your messages are
  private from them, like your other texts.
- **Your sponsor DOES see every decision**: what was requested, whether it was
  approved or declined, and which rule applied. So they won't see *how* you asked,
  but they will see *what* was asked for and what happened.
- The research team can see the conversation (see the privacy notice), because we
  need it to study how the system behaves. We treat it confidentially.

We will ask you to explain this back to us on the call, to make sure it's clear.

## Honesty and your comfort

- Steward is AI and **will sometimes get things wrong** — say something confusing, or
  make a wrong decision. Telling us when that happens is exactly what the pilot is for.
- This pilot sits inside a real relationship. If anything about it ever feels
  uncomfortable — including anything about how your sponsor uses it — you can tell us
  privately. We will not pass anything to your sponsor without your agreement, and the
  mid-pilot check-in is a private space to raise anything at all.

## Stopping

- Text STOP to steward, or contact us, at any time. It switches off for you within 24
  hours. **You do not need your sponsor's permission and you never need to give a
  reason.** If you stop, the pilot simply ends for both of you; your sponsor is told
  only that the pilot has ended.
