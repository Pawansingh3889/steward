## What steward is

Steward is an experimental, AI-powered assistant. The person you sponsor (the
"spender") texts it about things they need. Steward checks each request against
spending rules that you set, and approves or declines it according to those rules.

**Important: no real money moves in this pilot.** Steward runs on simulated ("sandbox")
money. Nothing is actually purchased, and nothing will arrive. The pilot tests whether
the *decisions and conversations* work — not real shopping.

## What you will do

- A 30-minute onboarding call with us, where we set up your rules and connect the
  spender's phone.
- Use the dashboard to see steward's decisions and change your rules whenever you like.
- Some weeks we will ask you to try a specific thing (for example, change a rule).
- A short private check-in call mid-way, and a 30-minute interview at the end.

You will be paid [AMOUNT] for your time, regardless of how much you use the system.

## What you can and cannot see

This matters, so we ask you to repeat it back to us on the call:

- **You see every decision** steward makes — what was requested, whether it was
  approved or declined, and which of your rules applied.
- **You do not see the spender's conversation.** Their messages with steward are
  private, the same way their other text conversations are. Steward reports decision
  facts to you; it will not characterize or editorialize about the spender.

## Risks and honesty

- Steward uses AI and **will sometimes make mistakes** — a wrong decision, a confusing
  message. Part of the pilot is finding out whether you can spot and correct these.
- This system sits inside a real relationship. If anything about it causes friction
  between you and the spender, tell us — that is important information, not a failure
  on your part, and we can pause your pair at any time.

## Stopping

- Text STOP to steward, or contact us, at any time. We will switch it off for your
  pair within 24 hours. You never need to give a reason.
- The spender can stop it too, independently of you, and does not need your agreement.
  If either of you stops, the pilot ends for both of you.
